Guardrails enforced

  • No third-party disclosure (RBI FPC). Recycled, third-party and heavily-shared numbers are suppressed before dialling — the debt is never discussed with a stranger.
  • Purpose limitation (DPDP). Bureau/reference contact data used only for collections; location data within retention limits.
  • Explainability. Every score is tree-based and attributable; every suppression carries a reason code.
  • No harsher treatment on hardship. A low reach-score on a vulnerable borrower routes to restructuring / a human, never pressure.
  • Exploration budget & drift monitors. Keeps low-scored points from being starved of evidence; PSI & calibration watched in production.
